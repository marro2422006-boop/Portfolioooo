About

I've spent the last six years turning rough ideas into interfaces people actually enjoy using — from early-stage product sketches to production systems serving thousands of people a day.

My process borrows a lot from fieldwork: observe closely, document what's true, prune what isn't, and only then build. I care about pages that load fast, code that reads clearly, and details that reward a second look.
